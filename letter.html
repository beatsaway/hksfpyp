Hong Kong Science Fair

Letter to parents

From Kenneth — warm, clear wording for Hong Kong school parents.

Email

Polished version

Dear parents,

We are looking for motivated students who are interested in science and design, and who care about helping others, to join this year’s Hong Kong Science Fair.

It is an interschool competition where teams use creativity and scientific methods to design solutions for real-world problems. Students will have a valuable chance to develop their technical skills, science expertise, and design skills under the guidance of professionals.

If you are interested, please fill in the Google Form by 21 November. Here are the highlights of winning projects from last year for your reference.

Thank you for reading this email.

Best regards,
Kenneth
