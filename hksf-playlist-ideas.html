短片靈感庫｜2025–2026 Primary finalist playlist

點標題可看該短片（YouTube）。用題目重寫 Stage 1「創作靈感」；再看 examples 或開 template。

Important tip

Stage 1 inspiration ≠ Input–Process–Output

寫靈感時，重點是情境、受惠對象，以及為什麼對你重要／你為什麼選這題。不要寫成輸入→處理→輸出的設計說明。

01. 2337 記憶守護者 Memory Guardian

長者記性變差時，溫柔提醒認人、認藥、認日常，幫照顧家庭少一點挫敗。 Gentler memory cues for elders and caring families—recognising people, meds, and daily routines.

Inspiration CN

探望婆婆時，她有時把孫兒名字叫錯，連剛吃過的藥也要人再講一次，家人反覆提示到大家都累。我想為記性漸漸變差的長者，以及每天陪伴的照顧家庭寫靈感：讓日常提醒更溫柔，認人、認事少一點挫敗。選這題，是因為我見過焦慮寫在家人臉上，仍希望長者覺得被尊重，而不是被考問。

Inspiration EN

When I visit grandma, she sometimes mixes up our names and needs the same medicine reminder again; family prompt until everyone is tired. I want inspiration for elders whose memory is fading and for the households that care every day—gentler cues, less failure when recognising people and routines. I chose this because I saw worry on family faces, yet elders still deserve respect, not a quiz.

02. 2343 最佳「智」友，「語」你同行！ Speech Buddy

給需要言語練習的小朋友同伴感，讓開口練習少孤單、敢慢慢說。 A speech-practice companion so kids feel less alone and can speak step by step.

Inspiration CN

有同學練習說話時臉紅結巴，一個人對鏡子練又悶，練兩句就想放棄，家人在旁也不知怎鼓勵。我想為需要言語練習的小朋友，以及陪伴練習的家人寫靈感：讓開口有同伴感，敢慢慢說。選這題，是因為我見過練習時的孤單，希望進步可以一點點累積，而不是一次就要完美。

Inspiration EN

Some classmates blush and stammer in speech practice; solo mirror drills feel dull, so they quit after two lines, and family do not know how to cheer. I want inspiration for kids who need speech practice and the relatives who coach them—so speaking feels less lonely. I chose this because I saw that loneliness; progress should stack in small steps, not demand perfection once.

03. 2359 雪櫃「鮮」生 Mr. i-Fridge

開櫃前大概知食物是否仍新鮮，幫長者與忙碌家長少吃壞、少浪費。 Fridge freshness cues so elders and busy parents waste less and eat safer.

Inspiration CN

家裡雪櫃深處常有水果變壞才被發現，公公有時仍拿起來咬，媽媽每次都很擔心食物安全，也心疼浪費。我想為家中長者與忙碌家長寫靈感：開櫃前就大概知道東西是否還能吃。選這題，是因為我不想家人吃壞肚子，也不想新鮮食物白白丟掉；小小提醒，能讓廚房安心一點。

Inspiration EN

Deep in our fridge, fruit often spoils before anyone notices; grandpa still bites it sometimes, and mum fears food safety while hating the waste. I want inspiration for elders and busy parents who should know freshness before digging inside. I chose this so family avoid upset stomachs and fewer good foods are thrown away—a small cue that makes the kitchen feel safer.

04. 2360 穩步前行 Steady step

為步履不穩的長者與陪行家人，起步、轉彎、停下時多一點安心。 Steadier starts, turns, and pauses for unsteady walkers and the relatives who escort them.

Inspiration CN

在商場我見過長者走路忽然不穩，家人要一直扶着，兩邊說話都輕輕的，生怕再晃一下。我想為步履不穩的長者，以及天天陪行的家人寫靈感：起步、轉彎、停下來時多一點安心。選這題，是因為我想公公婆婆仍敢出門買菜會面，家人不用全程提心吊膽，散步可以重新變得普通。

Inspiration EN

In the mall I saw elders suddenly unsteady while family held them the whole way, both sides speaking softly for fear of another wobble. I want inspiration for unsteady walkers and the relatives who escort them—safer starts, turns, and pauses. I chose this so grandparents still dare go out for groceries and chats, and carers need not fear every step; walking can feel ordinary again.

05. 2370 握住健康 Grip the Health

用更願意每天做的握力練習，幫手力變弱的長者保持自理。 Grip practice elders will actually repeat—supporting self-care when hands grow weaker.

Inspiration CN

探訪長者中心時，公公開瓶蓋、擰毛巾都很吃力，復康動作又悶，做幾下就說『下次啦』。我想為手力變弱、想保持自理的長者寫靈感：讓握力練習願意每天多做一點，家人也較易一起鼓勵。選這題，是因為手力關係倒水、開門、穿衣；我想他們少求人，尊嚴留在自己手裏。

Inspiration EN

At the elderly centre, grandpa struggled with jar lids and wringing towels; rehab felt dull, so he said ‘next time’ after a few tries. I want inspiration for elders with weaker hands who still want self-care—grip practice they will repeat, with family cheering along. I chose this because hand strength means pouring, doors, and dressing; I want them asking for help less, dignity kept in their own hands.

06. 2377 聲中「衣」星 The Dressing Star

清楚穿衣步驟，幫幼童自理更衣，也讓趕出門的家長早上少吵。 Clearer dressing steps for young kids learning self-care—and calmer school-run mornings.

Inspiration CN

弟弟早上更衣常前後穿反、鈕扣扣錯位，一趕時間就發脾氣哭，爸媽也跟着慌。我想為初學自理的幼童，以及趕着出門的家長寫靈感：用清楚步驟讓穿衣少錯、少吵。選這題，是因為我想小朋友慢慢靠自己穿好衣服，家人早上可以較平和，一整天不用帶着脾氣開始。我也想弟弟長大後記得：自己穿好衣服是一件值得驕傲的小事。

Inspiration EN

My little brother often wears clothes back-to-front or misses buttons; when we rush he cries, and parents panic too. I want inspiration for young kids learning self-dressing and for parents racing school mornings—clearer steps, fewer mistakes and fights. I chose this so children gradually dress themselves, mornings stay calmer, and the day does not begin with a bad temper for everyone.

07. 2403 綠色守護者 - 路燈空氣淨化系統 EcoDefender

路燈附近空氣淨化構想，照顧天天在路邊等燈的學生與街坊。 Cleaner air near street lamps for students and neighbours who wait by the road every day.

Inspiration CN

放學在路邊等過馬路，巴士尾氣一股一股，低年級同學會掩口鼻，老師也催快點過。我想為天天在路邊等車、等燈的學生與街坊寫靈感：路燈附近空氣能否好一點。選這題，是因為回家路不該等於吸髒空氣；我希望行人等紅燈時，仍能較自在地呼吸。每一個等燈的孩子，都應該吸到比較乾淨的空氣。

Inspiration EN

Waiting to cross after school, bus fumes come in waves; younger kids cover their noses while teachers hurry us. I want inspiration for students and neighbours who wait by the road every day—can air near street lamps feel cleaner? I chose this because the walk home should not mean breathing dirty air; people waiting at red lights deserve a freer breath.

08. 2420 RFID 智慧引路徑 RFID Smart Guide Path

Inspiration CN

社區活動見到視障朋友靠白杖慢慢探路，轉角與路口特別不安，陪同義工也要不停口述。我想為視障朋友與陪同者寫靈感：在常走路線上給更清楚的引導，少一點每步猜測。選這題，是因為熟悉的路也應走得有把握；我希望他們出行更獨立，陪同者也可以放心一點。出行不該每次都像考試，熟悉的路也值得被好好引導。

Inspiration EN

At a community event, visually impaired friends explored slowly with white canes, uneasy at corners while volunteers kept describing everything. I want inspiration for them and companions—clearer cues on familiar routes, less guessing each step. I chose this because even known paths should feel sure; I hope they travel more independently, and helpers can relax a little too.

09. 2421 智舒展：居家智慧服健助手 SmartStretch

居家伸展有提醒、做得正確，幫長坐上班的家人願意堅持。 Home stretch reminders and safer form for desk-bound family who only notice aches after work.

Inspiration CN

爸媽返工後腰肩又酸又緊，說要伸展，卻常忘記姿勢與次數，有時還越拉越不舒服。我想為長時間坐着上班、回家才想起身體的家人寫靈感：居家伸展有提醒、做得正確、願意堅持。選這題，是因為放工後應該真正放鬆，不是敷衍兩下；我想家人少帶痛上班。身體不應只在痛的時候才被想起，日常伸展值得被認真對待。

Inspiration EN

After work mum and dad’s shoulders ache; they mean to stretch, yet forget pose and count, sometimes pulling until it hurts more. I want inspiration for desk-bound family who only notice their bodies at home—reminders, safer form, habits that stick. I chose this because rest time should really loosen them, not two rushed moves; I want fewer sore mornings before work.

10. 2507 行行出狀元 Stroke Champs

讓漢字筆畫練習更清楚，幫覺得『寫字差』的小學生重拾信心。 Clearer Chinese stroke practice so primary kids rebuild handwriting confidence.

Inspiration CN

寫字課有同學筆順亂、捺鉤東歪西倒，愈改愈亂，下課後也不想再練，覺得自己『寫字差』。我想為正在練漢字筆畫的小學生寫靈感：讓一筆一劃變得清楚，練習不再那麼可怕。選這題，是因為筆順學穩了，功課信心會回來；我不想同學因為寫字而覺得自己不行。寫字是每天的事，練習應該帶來成就感，而不是羞愧。

Inspiration EN

In writing class some friends scramble stroke order and their hooks wobble; the more they fix, the messier it looks, so they avoid practice and feel ‘bad at writing’. I want inspiration for primary kids learning Chinese strokes—each stroke clearer, practice less scary. I chose this because steady stroke habits bring homework confidence back; I do not want classmates judging their whole self by handwriting.

11. 2509 街泊路路通 Park & Easy Toll

找街泊位、繳費少混亂，幫駕駛者與同車乘客出門少發火。 Easier street parking and payment—less chaos (and temper) for drivers and passengers.

Inspiration CN

爸爸在街上找咪錶位轉來轉去，過鐘又罰錢，全家困在車裡發脾氣，出門氣氛一下子變差。我想為常泊街的駕駛者與同車乘客寫靈感：找位、繳費少一點混亂與冤枉錢。選這題，是因為我不想為泊車耽誤約定，也不想行程未開始就先吵架；路邊停車可以少一點火氣。出門前的心情很重要，我不想泊車成為全家的火藥引。

Inspiration EN

Dad circles for meter spots, then overtime fines make the whole car grumpy before we arrive. I want inspiration for street parkers and their passengers—finding spots and paying with less chaos and fewer unfair fees. I chose this because parking should not make us late or start arguments; kerbside stops can involve less temper for everyone in the car.

12. 2540 智能污衣籃 Smart Laundry Basket

污衣一放下就大致分好，幫忙碌家庭少重分、少着色。 Laundry roughly sorted as it drops—less re-sorting and colour-bleed stress for busy homes.

Inspiration CN

家裡污衣混成一籃，淺色深色、校服運動服全堆一起，媽媽要重分很久，有時還因放錯而着色。我想為忙碌家庭寫靈感：污衣一放下就大致分得清，家務少走冤枉路。選這題，是因為家務已經很累；我不想分類再變成吵架點，也不想乾淨衣服被染花。洗衣籃若能先幫忙分類，週末家務會少很多怨氣。

Inspiration EN

Our dirty laundry piles into one basket—lights, darks, uniforms, sports kit—and mum re-sorts forever, sometimes colours bleed from mistakes. I want inspiration for busy families so clothes are roughly sorted the moment they drop. I chose this because housework is tiring enough; sorting should not spark arguments, and clean clothes should not get stained.

13. 2545 大型災害急救箱 Intelligent Disaster First Aid Kit

危急時知取什麼、怎處理小傷口，幫校園與家庭應變少慌亂。 Clearer grab-and-treat guidance for school and family first-aid readiness in a crisis.

Inspiration CN

地震演習後同學七嘴八舌問急救箱在哪、紗布膠布怎用，大家答不出，只記得要蹲低。我想為校園與家庭的應變準備寫靈感：危急時知道取什麼、怎處理小傷口，步驟清楚。選這題，是因為慌亂時最需要指引，不是更多驚嚇；我想同學在害怕時仍做得到基本照顧。演習不是走過場，真正用得着時，清楚步驟能救人。

Inspiration EN

After earthquake drills classmates asked where the first-aid kit was and how gauze and plasters work—nobody knew, only ‘duck and cover’. I want inspiration for school and family readiness: in a crisis, know what to grab and how to treat small wounds with clear steps. I chose this because panic needs guidance, not more fear; kids should still manage basic care when scared.

14. 2551 聲著 clothmate

選衣配色多一點自己做主，幫視障／弱視朋友保有穿着尊嚴。 More independent outfit matching for visually impaired friends—dressing with dignity.

Inspiration CN

義工活動認識視障叔叔，他說摸得到布料厚薄，卻不知顏色深淺，配衫要靠家人一句句講，有時會尷尬。我想為視障／弱視朋友寫靈感：選衣配色可以多一點自己做主。選這題，是因為穿着關乎尊嚴與心情；我不想他們每次出門都要問人『襯唔襯』。顏色與搭配若能自己掌握，出門會自信很多。

Inspiration EN

At volunteering, an uncle with low vision said he can feel fabric thickness but not colour, so outfits need family describing every piece—sometimes awkward. I want inspiration for visually impaired friends who deserve more choice matching clothes. I chose this because dressing is dignity and mood; I do not want them asking ‘does this match?’ every single morning before leaving.

15. 2580 「智引求生」火災逃生系統 i-Escape

Inspiration CN

火警演習煙霧提示一響，弟弟呆住，不知該走哪層樓梯，大人喊來喊去更亂。我想為家庭與大廈住戶寫靈感：濃煙或慌亂裡仍知哪個方向較安全。選這題，是因為真正火警不能靠猜；我想親人有一條平時就記得住的路，而不是臨時才吵架該左轉還右轉。平時多記一條路，危急時就能少一分混亂。

Inspiration EN

When the fire-drill smoke cue sounded, my brother froze—unsure which stairs—while adults shouting made it worse. I want inspiration for families and building residents who still need a safer direction in smoke or panic. I chose this because real fires allow no guessing; loved ones need a path practised in memory, not a last-second argument about left or right.

Inspiration EN

After school many bags bulge out of shape; one strap slips, kids complain of shoulder pain, and parents blame ‘messy packing’. I want inspiration for primary students and posture-worried parents—early warnings when bags are too heavy or uneven, with less blame. I chose this because we carry bags daily; spines should not suffer, and weight should not start home fights.

17. 2597 易時泊 Easy Park

快點知哪層有位、何時可能離開，幫屋苑住戶與訪客少兜圈。 Faster open-spot cues in estate car parks for residents and guest drivers.

Inspiration CN

屋苑停車場常要轉幾圈才有位，訪客更不知道哪層有空位，爸爸一邊開車一邊打電話問管理員。我想為住戶與訪客司機寫靈感：快點知道可泊之處、何時可能要走。選這題，是因為泊車耗油又耗脾氣；我想回家可以準時上樓，而不是在地庫兜圈生悶氣。地庫不該變成發脾氣的地方，回家應該先鬆一口氣。

Inspiration EN

I want inspiration for residents and guest drivers—see open spots and likely leaving times faster. I chose this because hunting parking burns fuel and tempers; I want us upstairs on time, not sulking in basement loops.

18. 2600 智識食 AI Dietary Consultant

Inspiration CN

婆婆有血壓問題，餐桌上常問『呢樣可唔可以食』，家人翻包裝字又細又多，誰也不敢肯定，飯菜都涼了。我想為有飲食限制的長者與照顧者寫靈感：選食物少一點猜測與害怕。選這題，是因為吃錯會怕，連聚餐都縮手；我想吃飯重新變得安心、有笑容。一餐飯的安心，對慢性病家庭來說已經很大。

Inspiration EN

Grandma has blood-pressure limits and keeps asking if a dish is allowed; family squint at tiny labels, nobody sure, while food goes cold. I want inspiration for elders with diet rules and carers—choosing food with less guessing and fear. I chose this because worry about eating wrong spoils gatherings; I want meals to feel safe and smiling again.

19. 2609 環環相救 VibraC

用震動傳『我在／需要幫忙』，幫聽障同學在嘈雜操場少誤會。 Vibration cues for hard-of-hearing classmates—‘I’m here / need help’ on a noisy playground.

Inspiration CN

聽障同學在嘈雜操場有時聽不到呼叫，獨自一角會慌，同伴以為他故意不理人。我想為聽障／弱聽朋友與同學夥伴寫靈感：用震動把『我在／需要幫忙』傳出去，少誤會。選這題，是因為安全與友誼都不應只靠聲音；我想大家在操場上互相找得到、幫得到。操場上每個同學都該被看見、被聽到，即使不用耳朵。

Inspiration EN

A hearing-impaired classmate sometimes misses shouts on a noisy playground and panics alone, while peers think he is ignoring them. I want inspiration for hard-of-hearing friends and classmates—vibration carrying ‘I’m here / need help’, fewer misunderstandings. I chose this because safety and friendship should not rely on sound alone; we must find and help each other on the field.

20. 2620 保寶媬 See See Care

短離席仍大概知寶寶狀況，幫新手父母與臨時照顧者少嚇出冷汗。 Short-absence baby status for new parents and stand-in carers—fewer cold-sweat moments.

Inspiration CN

親戚湊初生嬰，大人只去倒一杯水的一會兒也心驚，怕寶寶踢被、哭太久或吐奶沒人知。我想為新手父母與臨時照顧者寫靈感：短離席仍大概知寶寶狀況，少一點嚇出冷汗。選這題，是因為照顧也需要喘息；我想大人敢做必要的事，寶寶仍被好好看顧。照顧者有片刻安心，寶寶也會被照顧得更好。

Inspiration EN

When relatives care for a newborn, even pouring a glass of water feels scary—blankets slip, long cries, unnoticed spit-up. I want inspiration for new parents and stand-in carers who still need the baby’s status in a short absence, with fewer cold-sweat moments. I chose this because carers need breathers; adults must do needed tasks while babies stay properly watched.

21. 2628 高智能雨傘 High-tech Smart Umbrella

I want inspiration for rescue training and fire education: probe risky spaces in safer ways first. I chose this because people who save others also need protection; tech should lower their risk, and kids should know respect sits on real danger.

43. 3141 全聲導 Sound & Safe

關鍵方向用聲音說清楚，幫視障朋友在嘈雜商場仍有方向感。 Clear sound cues for key directions—orientation for visually impaired friends even in noisy malls.

Inspiration CN

參觀時見到視障朋友靠環境聲音認路，一到嘈雜商場就易迷失，陪同者要不停口述方向。我想為視障人士與陪同者寫靈感：關鍵方向用聲音說清楚，熱鬧地方仍有方向感。選這題，是因為安全應聽得到；我想他們在人多處仍知該往哪裡，而不是只能停在原地等幫忙。熱鬧地方若仍有方向感，出行勇氣會大很多。

Inspiration EN

On a visit, visually impaired friends used ambient sound to orient, yet noisy malls confuse them and companions must narrate nonstop. I want inspiration for them and helpers—clear sound cues for key directions, orientation even in busy places. I chose this because safety should be hearable; they deserve to know where to go in crowds, not only freeze and wait for help.
