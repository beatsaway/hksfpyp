This year’s Fair

A chance for your child to build something that helps people

Teams use science and design on a real project, with guidance along the way. Shortlisted and medal teams also gain recognition and Student Development Funds (Gold is HK$10,000 per student).

For parents

Why this matters

Students practise technical and design skills, think through problems as a team, and make something for a real audience — not only a classroom exercise.
