Teachers first

Stage 1 ≠ Stage 2

Stage 1（報名／申請）要短、要清楚。 表格問的是：項目中英名稱、主題、主要受惠對象、創作靈感（中／英）。不是完整 I/O 流程、詳細設計管線、驗證計劃全文、零件清單或程式架構——那些屬 Stage 2 項目計劃／提案（約 28 Feb 2027）才展開。
Stage 1 寫法目標： 讓評審／篩選者一秒看懂「誰有痛點、你們用創科改什麼、為何值得繼續」。靈感欄留下之後 Creativity（遊戲化／控制器巧思）與 Design & Validation（可在校測試）的伏線即可，不要在申請表寫成論文。

Stage 1 欄位對照

Project Name CN中文名：一讀就懂受惠／功能，可帶輕微雙關。 Project Name ENEnglish name：短、可念、與中文同義域。 Theme只選一個：Smart Living／Education／Medical & Healthcare／Smart City／Sustainability／Creative Solutions。 Expected Beneficiary表格選項常見：Children；Teenagers/Student；Family；General Public；People with disability；The elderly；Chronic patients；Particular occupation；Other。選最核心一個。 Inspiration CN約 120–150 字：場景痛點 → 觀察 → 為何用創科 → 期望改變（一句）。 Inspiration EN約 50–70 words：Who suffers → what fails → tech approach → hoped impact。與中文同義，勿只譯口號。

Example pack 1 · classmates

腳踏實學｜FootRight Coach

ThemeEducation BeneficiaryTeenagers/Student（次要：Children；Family）

Inspiration CN

課堂上不少同學上半身坐姿尚可，但雙腳懸空或歪放，長期不利骨骼發育。我們在班上觀察並做小訪談後，發現提醒一聲就過，很難堅持。因此想用 micro:bit 感測踏板／椅腳受力，配上短遊戲獎勵正確腳位，讓同學在日常上課中養成「腳踏實地」的習慣；詳細前後測留待 Stage 2。約 138 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

In class, many students look upright yet leave their feet dangling or twisted, which can hurt growing bones. After watching classmates and short talks, one-off reminders faded fast. We plan a micro:bit foot-pressure coach plus a tiny reward game so correct seating becomes a habit; school before-and-after checks come in Stage 2.~58 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits Primary micro:bit strength: 控制器＋課堂遊戲；本校易取樣 → 後期 Creativity／Validation 路子清楚，Stage 1 只寫觀察與方向。

Example pack 2 · children / public

分得清｜SortRover

ThemeSustainability BeneficiaryChildren（次要：General Public；Teenagers/Student）

Inspiration CN

校園回收常因分類不清而挫敗，同學也嫌說明沉悶、記不住。我們想先用 App／小遊戲教物料分類，再用 micro:bit 小車把「示範垃圾」送到正確回收格，讓低年級同學玩完就懂分、記得住。申請階段只說清問題與做法；詳細路線、規則與測試留待 Stage 2。約 126 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

School recycling often fails when sorting is unclear and posters feel dull. We want a short learn-then-sort game, then a micro:bit rover that carries sample waste to the right bin so younger pupils practise correctly and remember. This Stage 1 note only frames the need and approach; routes and tests come in Stage 2.~62 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits: 遊戲關卡＋Maqueen／線循車演示極強；受惠是兒童／公眾，不是長者敘事。

Example pack 3 · children

背得輕｜BagBuddy

ThemeSmart Living BeneficiaryChildren（次要：Teenagers/Student；Family）

Inspiration CN

不少小同學書包過重或單肩歪背，家長與老師都擔心姿勢與肩膀負擔。我們計劃用 micro:bit 加速度／拉力作即時提醒，再加每週小挑戰遊戲鼓勵「背得輕、站得正」，把糾正變成可玩的習慣。Stage 1 只交代痛點與控制器構想；荷重校準與同學測試屬 Stage 2。約 128 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

Many primary pupils carry overloaded or one-shoulder bags, and families worry about posture and shoulder strain. We propose a micro:bit tilt/load reminder plus a weekly challenge game that rewards lighter, balanced carrying so correction feels playable. Stage 1 only states the problem and controller idea; calibration and classmate trials belong in Stage 2.~58 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits: 對標書包類入圍題，但用現有加速／遊戲技能；校門量測即可鋪 Validation。

Example pack 4 · family / inclusive

走寶｜EscapeDrill

ThemeCreative Solutions BeneficiaryFamily（次要：Children；People with disability）

Inspiration CN

火警時家人常慌亂，兒童更難記住逃生選擇，演習又容易變成走過場。我們不做又一款純警報器，而想做「家居演練手掣」：情境卡＋ micro:bit 按鈕選正確路線，並可加震動／燈光模式照顧不同需要。申請表只寫清「演練＋共融」角度；感應與劇本細節留 Stage 2。約 142 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

In a fire scare, families panic and children forget escape choices, while drills can feel like going through the motions. Instead of another alarm-only gadget, we want a home drill controller: scenario cards plus micro:bit buttons to pick a safe route, with vibrate/light modes for different needs. Stage 1 frames the drill-and-inclusion angle; sensors and scripts wait for Stage 2.~64 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits: 火警題競爭多，差異化在「決策訓練＋共融」；控制器遊戲化對口 Creativity。

Example pack 5 · disability

拍拍同行｜BeatBuddy

ThemeEducation BeneficiaryPeople with disability（次要：Children；Family）

Inspiration CN

有語音或節奏學習需要的同學，單靠靜態練習難以保持專注，家人也難在家延續訓練。我們想用 micro:bit 按鈕／加速度做「拍拍節奏」控制器，配簡單 App 關卡練習跟拍與提示，讓訓練變得可玩、可重複。Stage 1 說明用家與靈感；個別化難度與專業意見屬 Stage 2 驗證。約 140 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

Classmates who need speech or rhythm support often lose focus in static drills, and families struggle to continue practice at home. We propose a micro:bit tap/motion controller with a simple app so practice becomes playable and repeatable. This Stage 1 note names the users and inspiration; personalised levels and specialist feedback are Stage 2 validation work.~55 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits: 共融受惠清晰；按鈕／加速度正是隊伍強項；避免與大量「認知障礙長者」題撞車。

Example pack 6 · elderly (one option among many)

力力更生｜GripQuest

ThemeMedical & Healthcare BeneficiaryThe elderly（次要：Family；Chronic patients）

Inspiration CN

探訪社區時見到公公婆婆因握力下降難開瓶蓋，復康運動又嫌枯燥而難以堅持。我們想用 micro:bit 感測握力，做成可換阻力的短訓練遊戲，讓長者在玩樂中完成每日練習，並把簡單進度給家人參考。此為六例之一，不是全校唯一方向；詳細訓練計劃與量度留 Stage 2。約 136 字 · Stage 1 長度

Inspiration EN

Visiting elders, we saw weaker grip make jar lids hard, while rehab felt boring and hard to keep up. A micro:bit grip sensor and a short adjustable-resistance game could support daily practice and share simple progress with families. This is one of six Stage 1 options—not the only school path—and the full training plan belongs in Stage 2.~64 words · Stage 1 length

Why it fits: 對標「握住健康」技能最贴；長者只是多元受惠中的一路。勿把全校策略做成 dementia-only。

School strategy

怎麼選，才不像「同一評審池撞車」

  • 分散受惠：同校多隊時，優先 FootRight／SortRover／BagBuddy／BeatBuddy／EscapeDrill；GripQuest 可留一隊。
  • 少做 dementia-only：證據難、與上年熱門敘事重疊、又較難展示 micro:bit 遊戲控制器優勢。
  • Stage 1 檢查表：名稱中英齊、Theme 單一、Beneficiary 核心一個、中英靈感達字數且同義、沒有把 Stage 2 管線塞進表格。
