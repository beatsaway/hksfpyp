Examples

腳踏實學｜FootRight

Education · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

我在班上數過，很多同學坐堂時腳懸空或蹺起。老師提過對骨頭不好，但提醒一次很快又忘記。我想用 micro:bit 放在腳下感測位置，腳擺正就有小遊戲分數，讓大家每天自己養成好習慣，不用老師一直喊。

Inspiration EN

In my class I counted many friends leaving feet hanging or twisted. Teacher said it is bad for growing bones, but one reminder fades fast. I want a micro:bit under the desk that gives a tiny game score when feet sit right, so we build the habit ourselves every day.

分得清｜SortRover

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

學校回收箱常常放錯東西。海報字很多，低年級同學記不住。我想先做分類小遊戲教大家，再用 micro:bit 小車把示範垃圾送到正確的桶。玩完就懂分，也記得住，校園回收會乾淨一點。

Inspiration EN

School recycling bins often get the wrong rubbish. Posters have too many words for younger kids. I want a short sorting game first, then a micro:bit rover that carries sample waste to the right bin. After playing, kids know how to sort and remember better.

背得輕｜BagBuddy

Smart Living · Children

Inspiration CN

放學時我見到很多書包鼓鼓的，有人只背一邊肩，回家說肩膀痛。爸媽也擔心姿勢。我想用 micro:bit 感應傾斜和重量，太重或不平衡就提醒，再加每週小挑戰，讓「背得輕」變成可以玩的習慣。

Inspiration EN

After school I see many heavy bags, sometimes on one shoulder, and friends say their shoulders hurt. Families worry about posture too. I want a micro:bit that senses tilt and load, reminds us when it is wrong, and adds a weekly challenge so carrying light feels like a game.

走寶｜EscapeDrill

Creative Solutions · Family

Inspiration CN

火警演習時，弟弟會慌，家人有時也忘了該走哪條路。我不想只做警報器。我想做家居演練手掣：情境卡加 micro:bit 按鈕，選對逃生路線才過關，還可以震動或燈光，讓不同需要的人都練得到。

Inspiration EN

In fire drills my little brother panics, and family sometimes forgets which way to go. I do not want another alarm only. I want a home drill controller with scenario cards and micro:bit buttons. Pick the safe route to pass, with vibrate or light so different people can practise.

拍拍同行｜BeatBuddy

Education · People with disability

Inspiration CN

有同學練習說話節奏時容易分心，紙上練習很悶，在家也難繼續。我想用 micro:bit 按鈕或搖動做「拍拍」控制器，配簡單 App 關卡跟拍。練習像遊戲，可以重複玩，家人也較易幫忙。

Inspiration EN

Some classmates lose focus in speech-rhythm drills. Paper practice feels dull, and home practice is hard. I want a micro:bit tap or shake controller with a simple app, so practice feels like a game they can repeat, and family can help more easily.

多喝水｜SipAlert

Smart Living · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

夏天上課，很多同學整天很少喝水，下課才說口渴頭痛。老師提醒也容易忘。我想用 micro:bit 和水瓶感應，到時間未喝就溫柔提醒，再用小分數鼓勵定時喝水，大家身體會舒服一點。

Inspiration EN

On hot school days many friends barely drink water, then feel thirsty or have a headache later. Teacher reminders are easy to forget. I want a micro:bit bottle helper that gently reminds on time, plus a tiny score game so drinking regularly feels fun and we feel better.

排隊不擠｜LineCalm

Education · Children

Inspiration CN

體育課排隊時，後面的人常推前面，小同學會害怕甚至跌倒。老師一個人很難看全部。我想用 micro:bit 量前後距離，太近就發聲或亮燈，再加「保持空間」小遊戲，讓排隊更安全、更公平。

Inspiration EN

In PE lines, people push from behind. Younger kids feel scared and sometimes fall. One teacher cannot watch everyone. I want a micro:bit that checks space, beeps or lights if we are too close, and a short space game so lining up feels safer and fairer.

綠芽日記｜SproutLog

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

班裏種豆芽，有人忘記澆水，芽就乾了；有人天天問「要不要澆」。我想用 micro:bit 量泥土乾濕，再配簡單日記 App：澆水、量高度都有貼紙分數，讓照顧植物變成每天清楚的小任務。

Inspiration EN

Our class grew bean sprouts, but some forgot to water and they dried, while others kept asking when to water. I want a micro:bit soil check plus a simple diary app. Watering and measuring height earn stickers, so plant care becomes a clear daily quest.

過馬路｜LightWalk

Smart City · General Public

Inspiration CN

我在街上見到有小朋友過馬路不看燈，家長也很緊張。真馬路練習太危險。我想做安全練習遊戲：micro:bit 當手掣，螢幕顯示燈號與車流，選對時間才過關，先在課室練好習慣。

Inspiration EN

On the street I see some kids cross without watching the lights, and parents look worried. Real roads are too dangerous for practice. I want a safe game: micro:bit as controller, screen shows lights and cars, and you pass only when you choose a safe time—first in class.

午膳分對｜LunchSort

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

午飯後，剩菜、包裝和餐具常混在一起，值日生要花很久整理。我想做 micro:bit 感應站，把東西靠近就亮正確桶色，再記班分。分類又快又清楚，食堂也會乾淨一點。

Inspiration EN

After lunch, leftovers, wrappers, and cutlery often mix, and helpers spend a long time fixing it. I want a micro:bit sorting station that lights the right bin colour when you bring an item close, and keeps a class score so sorting is quick and clear.

書桌光｜DeskGlow

Smart Living · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

晚上溫習時，有時燈太暗，有時太刺眼，寫完作業眼睛很累。家人也不知怎樣調最好。我想用 micro:bit 量光度，太暗或太亮就提醒，再用小遊戲教大家調好書桌光線。

Inspiration EN

When I revise at night the light is sometimes too dim or too sharp, and my eyes feel tired after homework. Family is not sure how to set it. I want a micro:bit light sensor that reminds me, plus a short game that teaches how to set desk light well.

餵寵提醒｜PetPing

Smart Living · Family

Inspiration CN

家裏養狗，哥哥溫習、爸媽加班時，有時會忘記餵或加水。狗等得很可憐。我想用 micro:bit 做餵食提醒手掣，到時間亮燈震動，家人按確認才算完成，並記下誰幫忙，大家一起負責。

Inspiration EN

We have a dog. When brother revises and parents work late, we sometimes forget food or water. The dog waits sadly. I want a micro:bit reminder that lights and vibrates on time. Family tap confirm when done, and we see who helped so care is shared.

洗手歌｜WashBeat

Medical & Healthcare · Children

Inspiration CN

同學洗手常常太快，歌還沒唱完就停，老師說這樣洗不乾淨。我想用 micro:bit 播節奏，手要跟著拍夠時間才過關。洗手像打機，大家較願意洗夠久，也更乾淨。

Inspiration EN

Classmates often wash hands too fast and stop before the song ends. Teacher says that is not clean enough. I want a micro:bit rhythm coach: keep tapping with the beat long enough to pass. Washing feels like a game, so kids wash longer and cleaner.

安靜小手｜QuietHands

Education · Children

Inspiration CN

有同學上課時手停不住，自己也很想專心，但旁邊的人會受影響。我想做 micro:bit「安靜小手」控制器：需要動時給安全節奏遊戲，需要專心時用輕震動提醒手放好，讓大家較易一起學習。

Inspiration EN

Some friends cannot keep hands still in class. They want to focus, but neighbours get distracted. I want a micro:bit QuietHands controller: a safe rhythm game when movement helps, and a gentle vibrate when hands need to rest, so learning together is easier.

力力更生｜GripQuest

Medical & Healthcare · The elderly

Inspiration CN

探訪公公婆婆時，我見到他們開瓶蓋很辛苦，復康動作又悶，做幾下就不想做。我想用 micro:bit 感測握力，做成短訓練遊戲，讓練習好玩一點，家人也能看簡單進度，一起鼓勵。

Inspiration EN

When I visit grandparents, jar lids are hard for them, and rehab feels boring so they stop early. I want a micro:bit grip sensor and a short training game so practice is more fun. Family can see simple progress and cheer together.

雨傘回家｜UmbrellaHome

Smart Living · General Public

Inspiration CN

下雨天，同學常把雨傘忘在課室或特別室，回家才發覺要淋雨。我想用 micro:bit 和書包感應：天氣顯示下雨而且傘不在袋就提醒，再用小分數鼓勵「傘跟你回家」，少一點人挨濕。

Inspiration EN

On rainy days classmates often leave umbrellas in classrooms and only notice when they must walk home wet. I want a micro:bit bag check: if rain is likely and the umbrella is missing, it reminds you, plus a tiny score for bringing the umbrella home.
