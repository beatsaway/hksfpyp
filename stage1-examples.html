Stage 1 examples

Stage 1 · due 29 Nov 2026

Examples

16 packs. Different beneficiaries. Write inspiration like these—then fill the template.

Important tip

Stage 1 inspiration ≠ Input–Process–Output

寫靈感時，重點是情境、受惠對象，以及為什麼對你重要／你為什麼選這題。不要寫成輸入→處理→輸出的設計說明。

腳踏實學｜FootRight

Education · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

坐堂時我數過，很多同學腳懸空或蹺起，老師說對成長骨頭不好，提醒一次很快又忘。我想為每天久坐的同學寫靈感：讓「腳擺正」變成自己在意的小事，不用老師一直喊。選這題，是因為我見過大家被提醒時的尷尬，又希望我們長大時背脊仍挺得住——好習慣該由自己慢慢養出來。

Inspiration EN

In class I counted many friends with feet hanging or twisted; teacher said it hurts growing bones, yet one reminder fades fast. I want inspiration for students who sit long days—so sitting with feet right becomes something we notice ourselves. I chose this because I saw awkward silence after reminders, and I hope our backs stay strong as we grow.

分得清｜SortRover

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

學校回收箱常放錯東西，海報字多，低年級記不住，值日生要翻來翻去。我想為剛學分類的小朋友，以及每天收拾的同學寫靈感：讓「分對」變得清楚、好記，校園回收乾淨一點。選這題，是因為我不想地球變成垃圾桶；小小分類若大家真的懂，校園也能教出一份責任感。

Inspiration EN

School recycling bins often get the wrong rubbish; posters have too many words for younger kids, and helpers dig through the mess. I want inspiration for children just learning to sort, and for classmates who tidy every day—so sorting feels clear. I chose this because I do not want Earth treated like a dump; if kids understand, school teaches responsibility too.

背得輕｜BagBuddy

Smart Living · Children

Inspiration CN

放學時書包鼓鼓的，有人只背一邊肩，回家說肩膀痛，爸媽也擔心姿勢。我想為背重書包的小朋友，以及擔心姿勢的家人寫靈感：讓「背得輕、背得正」變成大家願意在意的日常。選這題，是因為我見過朋友揉肩的樣子，也聽過家人的擔心；書包不該偷走童年該有的輕鬆。

Inspiration EN

After school bags look swollen; some hang on one shoulder, friends say it hurts, and parents worry about posture. I want inspiration for children who carry heavy packs and for families watching how they walk—so carrying lighter feels worth noticing. I chose this because I saw friends rub sore shoulders; a schoolbag should not steal the ease of being a kid.

走寶｜EscapeDrill

Creative Solutions · Family

Inspiration CN

火警演習時弟弟會慌，家人有時也忘該走哪條路，大家只會跟着人潮跑。我想為家中小童，以及陪他們練習的家人寫靈感：讓逃生路線變成平常能練的事，慌亂時少一點空白。選這題，是因為我不想家人在真正緊急時才第一次想清楚；安全該先在平日練成習慣，而不是靠運氣。

Inspiration EN

In fire drills my little brother panics, and family sometimes forgets which way to go—everyone just follows the crowd. I want inspiration for young children at home and relatives who practise with them—so escape routes become ordinary to rehearse. I chose this because safety should be built on calm practice days, not only in a real emergency.

拍拍同行｜BeatBuddy

Education · People with disability

Inspiration CN

有同學練習說話節奏時臉紅結巴，紙上練習很悶，在家也難繼續，家人在旁不知怎鼓勵。我想為需要言語節奏練習的小朋友，以及陪伴的家人寫靈感：讓開口少一點孤單，敢慢慢進步。選這題，是因為我見過練習時的尷尬與想放棄；進步該可以一點點累積，而不是一次就要完美。

Inspiration EN

Some classmates blush and lose the beat in speech-rhythm drills; paper practice feels dull, home practice fades, and family do not know how to cheer. I want inspiration for kids who need speech-rhythm practice and relatives who coach them—so speaking feels less lonely. I chose this because progress should stack in small steps, not demand perfection once.

多喝水｜SipAlert

Smart Living · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

夏天上課，很多同學整天很少喝水，下課才說口渴頭痛，老師提醒也容易忘。我想為忙碌上課、容易忘記照顧身體的同學寫靈感：讓定時喝水變成自己在意的小事，少一點硬撐。選這題，是因為我也試過忍到頭昏；學習再忙，也不該把身體發出的訊號一直推到最後才理會。

Inspiration EN

On hot school days many friends barely drink, then feel thirsty or headachy later; teacher reminders are easy to forget. I want inspiration for students busy through lessons—so regular drinking becomes something we notice ourselves. I chose this because I have felt dizzy from waiting too long; the body’s signals should not always come last.

排隊不擠｜LineCalm

Education · Children

Inspiration CN

體育課排隊時後面常推前面，小同學會害怕甚至跌倒，老師一個人很難看全部。我想為排隊中的小朋友，以及維持秩序的老師同學寫靈感：讓「保持空間」變成大家懂、也願意守的公平。選這題，是因為我見過推擠之後有人哭；好玩的課不該變成誰力氣大誰就擠到前面去。

Inspiration EN

In PE lines, people push from behind; younger kids feel scared and sometimes fall, and one teacher cannot watch everyone. I want inspiration for children in queues and classmates helping keep order—so keeping space feels fair. I chose this because I saw someone cry after a shove; fun lessons should not reward whoever pushes hardest.

綠芽日記｜SproutLog

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

班裏種豆芽，有人忘記澆水芽就乾了，有人天天問要不要澆，大家對「照顧生命」仍模糊。我想為剛學種小植物、想把責任做好的同學寫靈感：讓澆水、觀察變成每天清楚的小任務。選這題，是因為我見過芽枯掉時的失望；若連小生命都顧不好，更難談愛護我們共同的大地。

Inspiration EN

Our class grew bean sprouts; some forgot water and they dried, others kept asking when to water—caring for something alive still felt fuzzy. I want inspiration for classmates just learning to grow small plants—so watering becomes a clear daily duty. I chose this because if we cannot tend a tiny life, caring for the wider earth feels harder.

過馬路｜LightWalk

Smart City · General Public

Inspiration CN

街上有小朋友過馬路不看燈，家長很緊張，真馬路練習又太危險。我想為正在學過馬路的小朋友，以及緊張看着的家人寫靈感：先在安全地方練出「看燈再走」的習慣。選這題，是因為我不想有人用受傷來換取教訓；過馬路該在平常就練成自然反應，而不是只靠運氣過關。

Inspiration EN

On the street some kids cross without watching the lights; parents look worried, and real roads are too dangerous for practice. I want inspiration for children learning to cross and for anxious families—so watching the light can be practised somewhere safe first. I chose this because crossing should become a calm habit, not a gamble.

午膳分對｜LunchSort

Sustainability · Children

Inspiration CN

午飯後剩菜、包裝和餐具常混在一起，值日生要花很久整理，食堂也更容易髒亂。我想為吃飯的同學，以及收拾到很晚的值日生寫靈感：讓分類又快又清楚，大家離開時桌子乾淨一點。選這題，是因為我做值日時翻過混雜的桶；吃飯本是開心的事，收尾不該變成一種懲罰。

Inspiration EN

After lunch, leftovers, wrappers, and cutlery often mix; helpers spend a long time fixing it, and the canteen feels messier. I want inspiration for classmates who eat and duty helpers who tidy—so sorting is quick and clear. I chose this because on duty I dug through mixed bins; lunch should stay joyful, not end as punishment.

書桌光｜DeskGlow

Smart Living · Teenagers/Student

Inspiration CN

晚上溫習時燈有時太暗、有時太刺眼，寫完作業眼睛很累，家人也不知怎樣調最好。我想為夜讀的同學，以及關心視力的家人寫靈感：讓書桌光線變成大家懂得照顧、肯調整的小事。選這題，是因為我試過揉着眼硬把整份作業寫完；成績重要，但眼睛也要陪我們走很長的路。

Inspiration EN

When I revise at night the light is sometimes too dim or too sharp; my eyes feel tired after homework, and family is unsure how to set it. I want inspiration for students who study late and relatives who care about eyesight. I chose this because I have rubbed my eyes and pushed on; grades matter, but our eyes have to last.

餵寵提醒｜PetPing

Smart Living · Family

Inspiration CN

家裏養狗，哥哥溫習、爸媽加班時，有時會忘記餵或加水，狗等在碗邊很可憐。我想為家中寵物，以及分擔照顧、卻常忙到忘記的家人寫靈感：讓餵食加水變成大家一起記得的責任。選這題，是因為寵物不會自己說「我餓了」；既養了牠，就不該只在自己方便時才忽然想起。

Inspiration EN

We have a dog; when brother revises and parents work late, we sometimes forget food or water, and the dog waits sadly by the bowl. I want inspiration for pets at home and family who share care—so feeding becomes a duty everyone remembers. I chose this because pets cannot say they are hungry; we should not only remember when convenient.

洗手歌｜WashBeat

Medical & Healthcare · Children

Inspiration CN

同學洗手常常太快，歌還沒唱完就停，老師說這樣洗不乾淨，病菌仍容易在班上傳開。我想為愛玩耍、卻容易略過清潔步驟的小朋友寫靈感：讓「洗夠久、洗乾淨」變成願意完成的小事。選這題，是因為班上有人咳嗽後仍隨便沖一下；健康習慣該好玩，也要真正做得夠澈底。

Inspiration EN

Classmates often wash hands too fast and stop before the song ends; teacher says that is not clean enough, and germs still spread easily. I want inspiration for playful children who forget the steps—so washing long enough feels worth finishing. I chose this because healthy habits should feel fun, and still be done properly.

安靜小手｜QuietHands

Education · Children

Inspiration CN

有同學上課時手停不住，自己也很想專心，但旁邊的人會受影響，大家都不舒服。我想為需要動一動才能專心的小朋友，以及坐在旁邊的同班同學寫靈感：讓課堂既包容不同需要，又彼此顧得到。選這題，是因為我不想只靠責罵完事；學習方式不同，我們仍該一起把課上好。

Inspiration EN

Some friends cannot keep hands still in class; they want to focus, yet neighbours get distracted, and everyone feels tense. I want inspiration for children who need movement to concentrate, and for classmates beside them. I chose this because learning styles differ, yet we still need to learn well together without only scolding.

力力更生｜GripQuest

Medical & Healthcare · The elderly

Inspiration CN

探訪公公婆婆時，開瓶蓋、擰毛巾都很吃力，復康動作又悶，做幾下就不想做。我想為手力變弱、仍想保持自理的長者，以及在旁鼓勵的家人寫靈感：讓練習願意每天多做一點，尊嚴留在自己手裏。選這題，是因為手力關係倒水、開門、穿衣；我不想他們事事都要求人幫忙。

Inspiration EN

When I visit grandparents, jar lids and wringing towels are hard; rehab feels dull, so they stop early. I want inspiration for elders with weaker hands who still want self-care, and for family who cheer them. I chose this because grip means pouring, doors, and dressing; I do not want them asking for help with everything.

雨傘回家｜UmbrellaHome

Smart Living · General Public

Inspiration CN

下雨天同學常把雨傘忘在課室或特別室，回家才發覺要淋雨，第二天又有人鼻塞咳嗽。我想為容易忘帶傘的同學，以及接放學時被淋濕的家人寫靈感：讓「傘跟你一起回家」變成出門前多看一眼的習慣。選這題，是因為我也試過淋成落湯雞；小小提醒，能少一點感冒與狼狽。

Inspiration EN

On rainy days classmates often leave umbrellas in classrooms and only notice when they must walk home wet; next day someone has a stuffy nose. I want inspiration for forgetful students and families picking them up—so the umbrella comes home with one more glance. I chose this because a small reminder can spare colds and that miserable walk.
